About Pastel Cafe

Pastel Cafe is an aesthetic cafe with a soft pastel concept that offers a variety of coffee, non-coffee drinks, and delicious meals. Designed with a warm and homie atmosphere, the cafe features a simple interior decorated with soft colors and small greenery that creates a calm and relaxing vibe. It is the perfect place to enjoy good food and drinks while relaxing, working, or spending quality time with friends in a cozy environment.
